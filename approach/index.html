Approach

How Stonecliff Capital takes a family from a first conversation to an approved citizenship or residence file.

Four stages

The order matters. Due diligence preparation comes before any investment, so that a problem surfaces while your capital is still yours to redirect.

  1. Private assessment

    A confidential conversation, by video or in person, about your family, your current nationalities and residence, and what you want a second status to solve. Nothing you tell us leaves the firm.

  2. Written recommendation

    You receive our advice in writing: the programme and route we recommend, the alternatives we considered and rejected, the full cost including government fees and taxes, and a realistic timeline.

  3. Due diligence file

    Before any investment is made we assemble identity, civil-status and source-of-wealth documents and test them against what the programme’s due diligence will ask. If there is a problem, it surfaces here.

  4. Investment and filing

    Funds move from your own account directly to the qualifying investment. Licensed counsel or the authorised agent files the application, and we track it through to approval, oath or biometrics where required, and the issue of documents.

Principles we hold to

The programme serves the plan

We start from what the family needs a second status to do, then look for the programme. A passport bought for a visa waiver alone is exposed the day that waiver changes.

Genuine documents, full values

Every figure declared in a file we manage is the real one. We do not take on applications that depend on adjusted valuations or altered records.

Written confirmation of current rules

Thresholds and conditions move. Before you transfer funds you receive the rules that apply to your file on that date, in writing.

Discretion

Client names, files and the fact of an engagement are confidential. We do not publish client lists or testimonials.
