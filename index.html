Services for private clients

Programme watch

Rules in this field change several times a year. These are the changes we are advising clients around now.

November 2026

Hungary moves to close its investor programme

A draft bill published on 4 November would remove the Guest Investor programme from Hungarian law from 2027. We no longer present it as an option for new clients.

October 2026

Argentina sets its terms

USD 350,000 to the Treasury or USD 800,000 in a seven-year bond, with applications due to open before year end. We are preparing files for families who want to be early.

May 2026

Portugal doubles the wait for citizenship

Naturalisation now takes ten years of residence for most nationals. The Golden Visa and permanent residence after five years are unchanged, but the plan needs re-reading.

Ongoing

The Caribbean adds a presence rule

Five programmes, one regional regulator, and 30 days of physical presence being introduced country by country. Which rule applies depends on when a file is submitted.

Our approach in detail

  1. Private assessment

    A confidential conversation about your family, your passports and your aims.

  2. Written recommendation

    The programme and route we advise, the alternatives we rejected, and the full cost.

  3. Due diligence file

    We document source of wealth and resolve questions before any investment is made.

  4. Investment and filing

    Funds move from your account to the qualifying investment; counsel files; we track to approval.

Most of the value we add comes before any money moves: choosing the right programme, and documenting where the wealth came from so the file survives due diligence. We would rather tell a family that a programme is wrong for them than file an application that should not be filed.
