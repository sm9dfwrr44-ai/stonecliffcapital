Malta Permanent Residence Programme

Permanent residence in an English-speaking EU member state, granted from the start rather than after years of renewals.

Overview

Malta’s programme grants permanent residence to non-EU nationals who lease or buy a qualifying home in Malta or Gozo, make set payments to the government and a donation to a registered charity, and show a minimum level of assets.

The whole family is covered by one application, and there is no minimum stay. Malta’s former citizenship by investment route has closed; this programme is residence only.

Qualifying routes

Lease route

A qualifying long-term lease, with government payments totalling EUR 99,000 and the charitable donation.

Purchase route

Purchase of a qualifying property, with the same government payments and donation.

Who it suits

Families who want a permanent European status with English as a working language, and who would rather commit a smaller sum outright than a larger sum to property.

What to check before committing

The government payments are a cost, not an investment. Applicants must also show assets of at least EUR 500,000, part of it in financial assets.

Permanent residence here does not by itself lead to Maltese citizenship.

How we handle it

Applications are filed only through agents licensed in Malta. We prepare the file and source-of-wealth evidence and coordinate the licensed agent and property arrangements.
