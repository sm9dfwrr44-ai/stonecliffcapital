Greece Golden Visa

A five-year, renewable residence permit through property, with no requirement to live in Greece.

Overview

Greece issues a five-year residence permit to non-EU buyers of qualifying property, renewable for as long as the property is kept. Holders can travel within the Schengen area without a visa.

The threshold now depends on where and what you buy, after reforms designed to move investor demand away from the most pressured housing markets.

Qualifying routes

Prime areas, EUR 800,000

Attica, Thessaloniki, Mykonos, Santorini and the larger islands, for a single property of at least 120 square metres.

Rest of Greece, EUR 400,000

A single property of at least 120 square metres elsewhere in the country.

Conversions and restorations, EUR 250,000

Commercial buildings converted to residential use, and restoration of listed buildings, anywhere in Greece.

Who it suits

Families who want a European foothold and Schengen travel without relocating, and who are happy to hold Greek property for the long term.

What to check before committing

Property bought under the programme cannot be used for short-term holiday letting. Factor that into any yield you are shown.

Greek citizenship requires years of genuine residence, tax presence and a language and civics examination. The Golden Visa alone does not lead there.

How we handle it

We help you decide whether Greece fits the family’s plan, then coordinate licensed Greek counsel for the purchase, the legal checks on the property and the permit application.
