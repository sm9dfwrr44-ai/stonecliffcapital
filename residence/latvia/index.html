Latvia residence by investment

The lowest entry point to a residence permit in the European Union.

Overview

Latvia issues a five-year temporary residence permit to investors in Latvian companies, property, bank capital or government securities. The equity route starts at EUR 50,000, with a one-off state fee on top.

The permit allows Schengen travel and is renewed annually by registration.

Qualifying routes

Company equity, EUR 50,000

Investment in the share capital of a qualifying Latvian company, plus a EUR 10,000 state fee.

Real estate, EUR 250,000

Property purchase, plus a state fee of five per cent of the value.

Who it suits

Investors who want Schengen access at modest cost and are comfortable with an active or semi-active business investment.

What to check before committing

Latvia restricts applications from citizens of certain countries. Check eligibility for your nationality first.

The low threshold reflects real business risk and administrative upkeep. This is a permit to maintain, not one to file and forget.

How we handle it

We assess eligibility and whether Latvia is the right tool, then coordinate licensed Latvian counsel for the company or property and the permit.
