Portugal Golden Visa

Still Europe’s lightest residence requirement, but citizenship now takes ten years, not five.

Overview

Portugal’s Golden Visa grants residence to investors who spend only about a week a year in the country. Property stopped qualifying in 2023; today the main routes are regulated investment funds and cultural donations.

A revised nationality law in force since 19 May 2026 raised the residence period for citizenship from five years to ten for most nationals, and seven for citizens of EU and Portuguese-speaking countries. The Golden Visa itself was not changed, and permanent residence remains available after five years.

Qualifying routes

Investment funds, EUR 500,000

Units in qualifying Portuguese funds that do not invest in residential property.

Cultural donation, EUR 250,000

A donation to arts or national heritage projects approved by the state.

Other routes

Research funding and job-creating business investment also qualify.

Who it suits

Families who value the option of permanent residence in the EU with almost no presence, and who can take a ten-year view on citizenship.

What to check before committing

Processing has been slow; allow well over a year from application to first card.

If the plan was a passport in five years, it needs revisiting. The longer timeline applies to nationality applications made from 19 May 2026.
