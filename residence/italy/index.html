Italy Investor Visa

Residence in Italy through financial investment rather than property, approved before you commit the capital.

Overview

Italy’s Investor Visa offers a two-year residence permit, renewable for three more, to people who invest in Italian companies, government bonds or philanthropic projects.

The application is assessed by a government committee first. You make the investment only after the visa has been approved and you have entered Italy.

Qualifying routes

Innovative start-up, EUR 250,000

Equity in a company on Italy’s register of innovative start-ups.

Italian company, EUR 500,000

Equity in a limited company incorporated and operating in Italy.

Government bonds, EUR 2,000,000

Italian government bonds held for at least two years.

Philanthropy, EUR 1,000,000

A donation to a project of public interest in culture, education, research or heritage.

Who it suits

Families who intend to spend real time in Italy and prefer liquid or business investments to buying property for a permit. Often considered alongside Italy’s flat-tax regime for new residents, which needs separate tax advice.

What to check before committing

The investment must be maintained for the life of the permit, and start-up equity carries real risk of loss.

How we handle it

We prepare the committee submission and source-of-funds evidence, and coordinate licensed Italian counsel for the visa, the investment and the permit.
