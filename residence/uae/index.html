UAE Golden Visa

Ten-year renewable residence in the Emirates through property, in a country with no personal income tax.

Overview

The UAE Golden Visa gives investors a ten-year residence permit that is renewable and not tied to an employer or sponsor. The most-used route is ownership of property worth at least AED 2,000,000.

The holder can sponsor a spouse and children, and the visa stays valid during long periods outside the country, which ordinary UAE residence permits do not allow.

Qualifying routes

Property, AED 2,000,000

One or more properties with a combined value of at least AED 2,000,000. Mortgaged and off-plan property can qualify under conditions set by each emirate.

Other categories

Separate Golden Visa categories exist for entrepreneurs, specialists and large depositors. We advise on these case by case.

Who it suits

Families who want a real base in a well-connected, low-tax country, and business owners who need UAE residence for banking and company purposes. It is a place to live, not a path to a passport: naturalisation is rare.

What to check before committing

Residence and tax residence are different things. A visa alone does not move your tax residence out of your home country; days, home and centre of life do.

Check the title and the developer before you buy to qualify. Hotel-room and fractional units often do not count.
