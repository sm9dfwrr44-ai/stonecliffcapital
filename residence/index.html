Residence by investment

Golden visa and permanent residence programmes in the UAE and Europe, compared on investment, presence rules and where they lead.

What residence by investment is

Residence programmes, often called golden visas, give an investor and family the right to live in a country. In the European Union they usually bring visa-free movement around the Schengen area as well. They do not give a passport, and the road from residence to citizenship is longer and less certain than brochures suggest.

How to compare programmes

Do you intend to live there? If yes, tax residence, schools and language matter more than the threshold. If not, look closely at how little presence the permit requires to stay valid.

What form does the investment take? Property in Greece and the UAE; funds or donations in Portugal; company shares or bonds in Italy; a contribution with a lease or purchase in Malta.

Is citizenship part of the plan? Naturalisation generally requires real residence, language and years of waiting. Portugal lengthened its wait to ten years in 2026. Treat citizenship as a possibility, not the product.
