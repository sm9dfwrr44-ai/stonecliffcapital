Cyprus permanent residence

Permanent residence in an EU member state through a single property purchase, with very little required presence.

Overview

Cyprus grants permanent residence to non-EU nationals who invest at least EUR 300,000, most often in a new home bought from a developer, and who can show a secure annual income from outside Cyprus.

The permit covers the spouse and dependent children and does not expire, provided the investment is kept and the holder visits at least once every two years.

Qualifying routes

New residential property, EUR 300,000

A first sale from a developer, plus VAT.

Other assets, EUR 300,000

Commercial property, shares in a Cypriot company with local staff, or units in a Cypriot investment fund.

Who it suits

Families who want a Mediterranean home in the EU, an English-speaking professional environment and a permanent status without renewals.

What to check before committing

Holders must confirm each year that they still own the investment and still meet the income requirement.

Cyprus ended its citizenship by investment programme in 2020. This route is residence only.
