Private clients

Advice and file management for families, entrepreneurs and their advisers, from programme selection to life after approval.

The work, in full

Each engagement is led by one adviser and scoped in writing before it starts.

Programme selection

We set every realistic programme against your current nationalities, family composition, tax position and what you need the status to do. You receive a written recommendation, including the options we would rule out and why.

Source-of-wealth preparation

Most refusals trace back to wealth that was real but poorly documented. We assemble the record, identify gaps while there is still time to close them, and present it in the form each government’s due diligence providers expect.

Application management

We coordinate licensed lawyers and government-authorised agents in the programme country, manage translations, legalisations and deadlines, and keep you informed at each stage until a decision is issued.

Qualifying investments

Where the route is property, bonds or funds, we review the asset with you before you commit: title, valuation, holding conditions and how you exit. In Türkiye our team sources and manages property directly.

Banking and relocation

A new status usually needs a bank account, an address and sometimes a company behind it. We arrange introductions and handle formalities in the UAE and Türkiye, and coordinate trusted counsel elsewhere.

After approval

Passports are renewed, children are born, holding periods end. We remain the family’s point of contact for renewals, additions to the file and the eventual sale of a qualifying investment.

Who we act for

Families planning for stability

Parents who want their children to hold a second nationality, a place to relocate to at short notice, or schooling and healthcare options that do not depend on one country.

Entrepreneurs and investors

Founders whose passport limits where they can travel, bank or sign. We weigh mobility, banking access and tax residence together, because fixing one often moves the others.

Advisers and family offices

Lawyers, wealth managers and single-family offices who want a specialist to run the migration file for a client while they keep the relationship. We work under your engagement or alongside it.

What we do not do

We do not hold client money, sell property as a broker, or give legal opinions. Investment funds go from your own account to the government, bank, fund or seller. Legal work is carried out by licensed lawyers in the relevant country.

We also decline files. If the source of wealth cannot be documented, or a programme will not deliver what you need, we say so at the assessment stage.
