Privacy

How we handle personal information you share with us.

This website does not use advertising trackers and its enquiry form does not store or transmit your details to our servers. The form prepares a message that you send yourself by WhatsApp.

Information you send us

When you contact us we receive what you choose to share: typically your name, contact details, nationality, family composition and the outline of your enquiry. We use it only to respond and, if you engage us, to advise you and prepare your file.

Sharing

We share client information only with the lawyers, authorised agents, banks and government authorities involved in your file, and only as the file requires. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and your rights

Enquiries that do not become engagements are deleted on request. You may ask at any time what we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it, subject to record-keeping duties that apply to client files.

Fonts

Typefaces on this site are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads.
