Argentina citizenship by investment

South America’s first citizenship by investment programme, announced in October 2026 and not yet open for applications.

This programme has been announced but is not yet accepting applications. Details below are as published by the Argentine government and may change before launch.

Overview

On 2 October 2026 Argentina’s government set out the terms of a citizenship by investment programme, built on decrees issued in 2025 that allow naturalisation on the basis of a relevant investment rather than years of residence.

The government has said applications will open during the fourth quarter of 2026. They will be assessed by a dedicated agency with input from intelligence, financial-crime and security bodies, and decided by the national migration directorate.

Qualifying routes

Treasury contribution, USD 350,000

A non-refundable contribution to the National Treasury for the main applicant. Family members are added for further contributions; a family of four comes to about USD 500,000.

Government bond, USD 800,000

Purchase of a public bond, to be held for seven years.

Who it suits

Families who want a second nationality from a large G20 economy with a strong passport, and who can wait for a new programme to settle. Those drawn by Spain’s shorter naturalisation period for Ibero-American nationals should take separate Spanish advice before relying on it.

What to check before committing

No application has yet been processed. Timelines, document lists and the due diligence standard will only be known once the first files are decided.

New programmes attract scrutiny from the European Union and others. Visa-free arrangements enjoyed by Argentine citizens today are not guaranteed for citizens by investment.

How we handle it

We keep a short list of families who want to be ready when applications open. There is no charge to join it. We use the time to prepare source-of-wealth files, which will be needed whatever the final procedure looks like.
