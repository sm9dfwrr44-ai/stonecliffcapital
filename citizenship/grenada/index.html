Grenada citizenship by investment

The Caribbean programme with a US E-2 treaty behind it, chosen most often by families with an eye on America.

Overview

Grenada offers citizenship for a contribution to its National Transformation Fund or an investment in approved real estate. Its distinguishing feature is a treaty with the United States that lets Grenadian citizens apply for the E-2 investor visa.

In 2026 Grenada legislated a presence requirement in line with the regional framework, then deferred its commencement shortly before it was due to start.

Qualifying routes

National Transformation Fund, from USD 235,000

A non-refundable contribution for a single applicant, with higher amounts for families.

Approved real estate

Investment in a government-approved project at a higher threshold, held for a minimum period.

Who it suits

Entrepreneurs who plan to run a business in the United States and need a treaty nationality, and families who want a wider definition of dependants than most programmes allow.

What to check before committing

US law requires citizens by investment to have been domiciled in the treaty country for three years before applying for an E-2 visa. Plan for that period.

The five Eastern Caribbean programmes now sit under a regional regulator, ECCIRA, and a requirement of 30 days of physical presence in the first five years of citizenship is being brought in country by country. Ask which rule will apply to a file submitted on your date.

Visa-free access to the Schengen area for Caribbean investor citizens is under review by the European Union. Do not choose a programme for a visa waiver alone.

How we handle it

Caribbean applications must be filed through agents authorised by the government concerned. We prepare the source-of-wealth file, brief you for the mandatory interview, coordinate the authorised agent and check every payment instruction before you transfer.
