Citizenship by investment

Seven programmes that grant a passport on the strength of an investment, compared on cost, conditions and what the citizenship is worth to a family.

What citizenship by investment is

A small number of countries grant citizenship to people who make a defined investment and pass background checks, without requiring them to live there first. The result is a full nationality: a passport, the right to live and work in the country, and usually the right to pass citizenship to children.

How to compare programmes

The headline minimum is the least useful number. Three questions separate programmes more sharply.

Is the money spent or invested? Caribbean programmes centre on a non-refundable contribution. Türkiye's routes are investments you hold for three years and may then sell.

What does the passport do for this family? Visa-free lists, treaty rights such as the US E-2 visa, and the right to settle in the country all differ, and visa waivers can be withdrawn.

What will due diligence ask? Every serious programme now tests source of wealth in depth, and the Caribbean programmes require an interview. Preparation, not price, decides most outcomes.
