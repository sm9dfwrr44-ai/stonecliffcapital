St Lucia citizenship by investment

The youngest of the five Eastern Caribbean programmes, with a contribution route and approved real estate.

Overview

St Lucia grants citizenship for a contribution to its National Economic Fund or an investment in approved real estate or enterprise projects.

It is party to the regional regulatory agreement with its four neighbours, and its rules are converging with theirs.

Qualifying routes

National Economic Fund, from USD 240,000

A non-refundable contribution for a single applicant, with higher amounts for families.

Approved real estate

Investment in a government-approved project at a higher threshold, held for a minimum period.

Who it suits

Applicants comparing the Caribbean programmes on overall cost for their particular family size, where St Lucia can come out ahead.

What to check before committing

The five Eastern Caribbean programmes now sit under a regional regulator, ECCIRA, and a requirement of 30 days of physical presence in the first five years of citizenship is being brought in country by country. Ask which rule will apply to a file submitted on your date.

Visa-free access to the Schengen area for Caribbean investor citizens is under review by the European Union. Do not choose a programme for a visa waiver alone.

How we handle it

Caribbean applications must be filed through agents authorised by the government concerned. We prepare the source-of-wealth file, brief you for the mandatory interview, coordinate the authorised agent and check every payment instruction before you transfer.

Figures are government minimums as published and were reviewed in November 2026. Programmes change without notice; we confirm current rules in writing before you commit funds.
