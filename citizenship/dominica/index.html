Dominica citizenship by investment

The lowest threshold among the Eastern Caribbean programmes, at the agreed regional floor of USD 200,000.

Overview

Dominica grants citizenship for a contribution to its Economic Diversification Fund or a purchase of approved real estate. Since the Eastern Caribbean states agreed a common price floor, Dominica sits exactly on it.

A family of four pays a higher contribution than a single applicant, so the comparison with neighbouring programmes narrows for larger families.

Qualifying routes

Economic Diversification Fund, from USD 200,000

A non-refundable contribution for a single applicant; USD 250,000 for a family of four.

Approved real estate, from USD 200,000

A share in government-approved property, held for a minimum period, plus government fees.

Who it suits

Single applicants and couples for whom cost is the deciding factor and who do not need visa-free entry to the United Kingdom.

What to check before committing

Citizens of Dominica have needed a visa to enter the United Kingdom since 2023.

The five Eastern Caribbean programmes now sit under a regional regulator, ECCIRA, and a requirement of 30 days of physical presence in the first five years of citizenship is being brought in country by country. Ask which rule will apply to a file submitted on your date.

Visa-free access to the Schengen area for Caribbean investor citizens is under review by the European Union. Do not choose a programme for a visa waiver alone.

How we handle it

Caribbean applications must be filed through agents authorised by the government concerned. We prepare the source-of-wealth file, brief you for the mandatory interview, coordinate the authorised agent and check every payment instruction before you transfer.

Figures are government minimums as published and were reviewed in November 2026. Programmes change without notice; we confirm current rules in writing before you commit funds.
