Türkiye citizenship by investment

Citizenship of a G20 country through an investment you hold for three years and may then sell. The programme our team knows best.

Overview

Türkiye grants citizenship to investors who place qualifying capital in the country and keep it there for three years. Unlike contribution-based programmes, every route is an asset you own: a property, a deposit, bonds or fund units.

The spouse and children under 18 are included in the same application. There is no requirement to live in Türkiye, no language test and no interview; the family visits once, briefly, to give fingerprints.

Qualifying routes

Real estate, USD 400,000

One or more properties with a combined value of at least USD 400,000, confirmed by an official valuation report and registered with a three-year no-sale annotation.

Bank deposit, USD 500,000

A deposit of at least USD 500,000 or its equivalent in a bank operating in Türkiye, blocked for three years. Interest is paid to the investor.

Government bonds, USD 500,000

Turkish government debt instruments to the value of USD 500,000, held for three years under the current Treasury rules on which instruments qualify.

Fund routes, USD 500,000

Units in qualifying investment funds or the private pension system, held for three years.

Who it suits

Families who want their capital back, or working for them, rather than spent on a contribution. Entrepreneurs who value a large domestic market and a base between Europe, the Gulf and Asia. Nationals of countries with limited travel freedom who want a meaningful step up without a residence obligation.

What to check before committing

Buy only property with a title deed that is ready and can be registered directly in the investor’s sole name. Applications based on sale-promise contracts stall in practice, and shared title does not qualify.

The valuation report must support the price actually paid. Files built on inflated or adjusted values are the ones that fail.

Türkiye is a US E-2 treaty country, but US law requires citizens by investment to have been domiciled in the country for three years before applying for an E-2 visa.
