About Stonecliff Capital

A Dubai-based private advisory with more than 500 citizenship applications behind its team.

The firm

Stonecliff Capital is a private advisory on citizenship and residence by investment. It is operated by Stonecliff Capital Consultancy FZCO, registered in the IFZA free zone at Dubai Silicon Oasis, United Arab Emirates.

We advise a limited number of families each year. The work is unhurried by design: a second citizenship affects children, estates and tax positions for decades, and it deserves more than a comparison table.

Experience

Our team has worked in investment migration since 2018 and has completed more than 500 citizenship by investment applications, most of them under Türkiye's programme, for families from Europe, the Middle East, Asia and beyond.

That experience sits in the part of the process that decides outcomes: establishing where wealth came from, presenting it so that it withstands government due diligence, and making sure the qualifying investment is one the family would be content to own anyway.

Independence

Stonecliff Capital is not affiliated with any government and cannot guarantee the outcome of an application; decisions rest with the authorities of each country. Applications are filed through government-authorised agents and licensed local counsel in each country, with Stonecliff coordinating the file.

Where to find us

Dubai Silicon Oasis, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Meetings in Dubai are by appointment; most engagements run by video call and secure document exchange.
